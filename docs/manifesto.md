# The SUNGUR Manifesto: Siber-Asabiyet in Aerial Autonomy

## Introduction
In the age of algorithmic warfare, the sky is no longer just a physical domain; it is a data-driven battleground. The **SUNGUR Tactical Interface** is not just software—it is a digital extension of the operator's intent, built upon the concept of *Siber-Asabiyet* (Cyber-Solidarity/Discipline).

## The Pillars of Siber-Asabiyet
1. **Technical Dominance**: We do not just use technology; we master its underlying physics. Our systems are designed to resist interference through cognitive resilience.
2. **Operational Discipline**: Every waypoint, every telemetry packet, and every control command is a reflection of strategic order.
3. **Decentralized Intelligence**: The swarm is the unit. Survival depends on the collective synchronization of autonomous agents.

## Technical Imperatives
- **GNSS-Denied Navigation**: Reliance on satellites is a vulnerability. SUNGUR prioritizes visual and inertial odometry.
- **Low-Latency Feedback**: The gap between perception and action must be minimized to the speed of light.
- **Electronic Stealth**: Active emissions are tracked and minimized to maintain the tactical edge.
